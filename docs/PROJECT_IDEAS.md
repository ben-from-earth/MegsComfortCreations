## Capstone Outline and Brainstorm

# Idea 1: Small Business Website

Proposal:

A website for one of my connections, who runs a small business on Etsy. Over the past few years, I've noticed several tasks that this person performs manually that could be automated to significantly boost their productivity and increase the number of orders they can complete, especially during the busy holiday season. To support the business, I plan to develop a dedicated website that not only showcases the shops products, but also includes a custom-built tool to assist with backend tasks, like collecting book cover images from Google searches or OpenLibrary, saving book data to a database, and converting the collected images and data into a format that the shop can easily use for orders. This tool will be accessible only to the shop owner via a secure login system.

I want to utilize all parts of the MERN stack to hold the data related to the personal tool, as well as track orders, repeat/common customers, provide subscribers to a newsletter, etc. I feel like this is a true win-win for both of the shop runner and me. I get to flex and hone my skills as a software developer and I get to make their job a little easier and provide some tools to grow the business.

# Idea 2: Three Card game Website

Proposal:

My friends and I play a card game called Three Card. The specifics of the game are not too important, but it’s a fun game for when we all get together. Unfortunately, its uncommon that we are all physically together. I want to build a site that we all (or anyone) can log into and play our game together over the internet. The functionality of the game itself isn’t too complicated, but it will really stress my skills in multi-user functionality. Im not too sure if that will be taught in this class or if this is better suited for an app (likely), but I like that it will push me to do some research into the topic. I want to at least build the groundwork for this with a website, and maybe later on I can spend some time building my app portfolio with this project.

# Idea 3: Budgeting Website

Proposal:

In this day where I feel like a lot of us are really spending a lot of time watching our money and trying to make it stretch as far as possible, I want to build a site where I can make it as easy as possible for someone to build a budget for their family. I feel like a lot of the apps out there that Ive seen cost a decent amount of money (seems a bit counter-intuitive to me) or simply require a lot of user input throughout every transaction you make.

I have found it extremely helpful to have an all in one excel sheet that tracks my family’s net worth over the year, our investments, how our money gets split up over bills and different categories like pets, food/gas, dates, etc. I want to provide something where any average joe with no budgeting experience can plug in a few known constants (paycheck + frequency, bills, what categories you want) and we can build out a helpful budget together.
